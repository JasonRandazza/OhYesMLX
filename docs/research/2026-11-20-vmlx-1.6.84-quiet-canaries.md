## Findings

1. **The TTFT rise is not confirmed.**
   - **Quiet runs.** The three quiet runs read 0.184–0.190 s. Their mean is 0.1878 s.
   - **Against 1.6.82.** The two 1.6.82 runs read 0.181 and 0.188 s, a mean of 0.1846 s. That puts 1.6.84 at +1.7%, below the canary's 5% noise floor, and inside the range 1.6.82 itself produced.
   - **The earlier "rise".** The earlier sequence 0.183 → 0.189 → 0.196 s was its highest reading on 11-19. It does not repeat on a quiet machine.

2. **There is no cold-load regression; the comparison in item 9 was a misread.**
   - **What item 9 compared.** Item 9, and `PHASE-5-SUMMARY.md` before it, compared 1.6.84's 15–22 s with "about 2.5 s at 1.6.82".
   - **What 1.6.82 actually recorded.** Both 1.6.82 canaries recorded a `cold_load_s` of **13.2 s**.
   - **Where the 2.5 came from.** The 2.4–2.6 in STATE's 2026-11-02 note was the machine's load average, written as "load 2.4-2.6".
   - **1.6.84 warm.** On a quiet machine 1.6.84 loads in **11.2 s**, twice. That is faster than 1.6.82's 13.2 s, by about 1.8 s of which only 0.07 s is S5-F2's version probe. Both 1.6.82 runs predate 05-07.
   - **First start of a session.** The first start of each session is slower: 22.2 s right after the upgrade on 11-19, and 17.2 s for the first run today. These look like cold page cache or first-launch work. They are not a version effect, and nothing here separates the two causes.

3. **The canary's "moved +5–7%" verdicts reflect a low baseline.**
   - **What was flagged.** All three runs read "moved" on decode: +7.0%, +5.3% and +7.2% against the recorded baseline of 73.40 tok/s.
   - **Why the baseline is low.** That baseline is the lowest of the six 1.6.84 readings, which span 73.4–80.7 tok/s; the three quiet ones span 77.3–78.7, a 1.8% spread.
   - **Against 1.6.82.** 1.6.84's decode reads higher than 1.6.82's (70.5 and 74.5). The 1.6.82 pair comes from two sessions that STATE already called "session variance at the 5% floor", so this is **not established** as a version effect.

## What this does not say

- That vMLX 1.6.84 decodes faster than 1.6.82. That would need both versions measured in one quiet session, and only 1.6.84 is installed now.
- Why the first start of a session is slower. That needs a page-cache-controlled probe (purge, then start), which is not part of this item.
- Anything about the other four engines.
