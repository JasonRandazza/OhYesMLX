# S2-F1 — a FAILED quickbench cell carries its tok/s and TTFT to the app

## Claim

`quickbench._cell_record` copies `decode_tps` and `ttft_p50_s` off `report.summarize`'s row with
no status test, so a FAIL cell (incoherent output, or some requests lost) reaches the app's
`result` event and `quickbench.json` carrying a tok/s and TTFT that both Swift cell views print
beside the word "Failed".

## Code path (read, not inferred)

- `ohyesmlx/report.py:829-893` — `_row` builds `ttft_p50_s` (850) and `decode_tps` (859) from
  the observations and never consults `result.status`; `status` is copied onto the row (835).
- `ohyesmlx/measure.py:1223-1251` — `_set_status` sets `FAIL` for incoherent output and for
  failed requests; it does not touch the observations, so their timings survive.
- `ohyesmlx/quickbench.py:750-751` — `rows = report.summarize(results)` then
  `return _cell_record(..., row=rows[0])` with no status gate.
- `ohyesmlx/quickbench.py:346-349` — `"decode_tps": (row or {}).get("decode_tps")` and
  `"ttft_s": (row or {}).get("ttft_p50_s")` under the docstring at 335-337: "a skipped or
  failed one carries its own status and reason with no number at all."
- `ohyesmlx/quickbench.py:1002` — the same dict is emitted as the `result` event payload.
- `app/Sources/OhYesMLXKit/AppModel.swift:98-104` — `BenchmarkCell.decodeTPS` is a plain
  `Double?`; no status gate.
- `app/Sources/OhYesMLX/Screens/ResultsView.swift:246-247` — `figure(cell.decodeTPS.map(Format.rate))`
  / `figure(cell.ttftS.map(Format.duration))`, unconditional on `cell.status`.
- `app/Sources/OhYesMLX/Screens/BenchmarkView.swift:341` — `if let decode = cell.decodeTPS`
  renders the figure, unconditional on `cell.status` (shown beside `status.word`).

Scope confirmed as the finding states: `_winner` (`quickbench.py:385`) and `_best`
(`quickbench.py:415`) both filter `status == "PASS"`, so the best-on-your-Mac card
(`ResultsView.swift:102`) and `Pick.measured_here` (`ResultsView.swift:178`) cannot receive a
failed cell's number. The defect is the per-cell record and its persistence/event surface.
